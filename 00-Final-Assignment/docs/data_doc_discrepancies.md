# Documentation vs. Data Discrepancies — Company A Telecom Dataset

> **Context (Task T1.6 & PRD §5.3):** Before building features or presenting results to executives, we audited every column in `telecom/Client.csv` and `telecom/Record.csv` against `ENG_Company _A_ Dataset Overview.docx` and `tutorial.ipynb`. Below are the verified discrepancies and the exact conventions adopted across our code and slide deck.

---

## 1. Summary Table of Verified Discrepancies

| Column / Item | What Official Documentation Says (`ENG_Company _A_ Dataset Overview.docx` / `tutorial.ipynb`) | What Actual CSV Data Shows (`Client.csv` + `Record.csv`, $N=100{,}000$) | Impact & Wording Adopted on Slides |
|---|---|---|---|
| **`change_mou`** | *"Percentage change in monthly minutes of use vs previous three month average"* | **22.79%** of non-null values ($22{,}588 / 99{,}109$) are **$< -100$** (min $= -3{,}875.0$), which is mathematically impossible for a percentage decline. Pearson correlation with absolute difference (`mou_Mean - avg3mou`) is **$r = 0.9025$**, whereas correlation with true percentage change is only $r = 0.1061$. | Treat as **absolute change in monthly minutes of use vs. prior 3-month average** (or unscaled delta). On slides, label as **"Change in Monthly Minutes (`change_mou`)"**, never "% change". |
| **`change_rev`** | *"Percentage change in monthly revenue vs previous three month average"* | **1.12%** of non-null values ($1{,}108 / 99{,}109$) are **$< -100$** (min $= -1{,}107.74$). Correlation with absolute difference (`rev_Mean - avg3rev`) is **$r = 0.9440$**. | Treat as **absolute change in monthly revenue (MU) vs. prior 3-month average**. Label on slides as **"Change in Monthly Revenue (`change_rev`)"**. |
| **`months` table location** | `tutorial.ipynb` §2.3 table states that `Client.csv` contains *"account-level info: tenure, plan, demographics, equipment"*. | `months` (total months in service, range 6–61, median 16.0) is actually stored in **`Record.csv`**, while lifetime totals (`totcalls`, `totmou`, `totrev`, `avg*`) are in `Client.csv`. | Always inspect merged `df.columns` and cite `Record.csv` as the source of `months` in `outputs/tables/column_map.csv`. |
| **`recv_sms_Mean`** | Description is **`NaN`** (blank in Overview table). | Present in `Record.csv`; **99.13% zeros** (only 872 customers have non-zero values). | Near-empty column with negligible signal; documented in data audit and excluded from key business levers. |
| **`iwylis_vce_Mean`** | Description is **`NaN`** (blank in Overview table). | Correlates **$r = 0.7855$** with `mouiwylisv_Mean` (*"Mean unrounded minutes of use of inbound wireless to wireless voice calls"*) and **$r = 0.7279$** with `owylis_vce_Mean` (*outbound wireless-to-wireless voice calls*). | Inferred from naming pattern and correlation as **"Mean number of inbound wireless-to-wireless voice calls"**; explicitly noted as an analytical inference, not official confirmation. |
| **`Customer_ID`** | Description is **`NaN`** in Overview table (and referred to as `ID_Customer` in some legacy notes). | Exact column name is **`Customer_ID`** in both `Client.csv` and `Record.csv`; unique integers `1000001` to `1100000` in identical row order (1:1 inner join). | Join on `Customer_ID` with shape and uniqueness assertions, then drop before modeling to prevent memorization. |
| **`hnd_price`** | *"Current handset price"* (implies continuous currency). | Takes only **17 discrete price tiers** (`9.99, 29.99, 39.99, ..., 499.99`), with 847 missing rows (0.85%). | Treat as discrete handset price tiers (e.g., entry-level $\le 29.99$ MU vs. premium $\ge 149.99$ MU). |
| **`infobase`** | *"InfoBase match"* | Only two non-null values: `'M'` ($77{,}697$ rows, churn $48.92\%$) and `'N'` ($224$ rows, churn $33.04\%$), plus $22{,}079$ missing rows (churn $51.98\%$). | Represents whether the customer record matched an external third-party demographic database (Acxiom InfoBase). Not an actionable business lever; its feature importance reflects demographic match availability. |
| **Negative values in positive quantities (`eqpdays`, `rev_Mean`, `totmrc_Mean`, `avg6rev`)** | Described as positive counts/amounts (*age in days, mean monthly revenue, recurring charge*). | `eqpdays` has **133 negative rows** (down to $-5$ days, including 34 rows equal to $-1$; churn rate $35.34\%$). `rev_Mean` has **5 negative rows** (min $-6.17$); `totmrc_Mean` has **23 negative rows** (min $-26.92$); `avg6rev` has **3 negative rows** (min $-2.0$). | Causes `RuntimeWarning: divide by zero / invalid value encountered in log1p` in `tutorial.ipynb` Cell 29 when `np.log1p` is applied to $-1$ or $< -1$. In our pipeline, we create explicit negative/anomaly flags (`eqpdays_negative_flag`) and clip `eqpdays` at $0$ before computing ratios or log transforms. |
| **Currency & observation dates** | Overview mentions *"charge amount"* and *"31–60 days after observation date"*, with a typo *"Company I's strict data protection policies"*. | No currency symbol or calendar timestamps exist anywhere in the files. | Express all monetary figures in **Monetary Units (MU)** and explicitly note the absence of calendar timestamps (preventing seasonal or out-of-time backtesting) on Slide 4 and Slide 14. |
