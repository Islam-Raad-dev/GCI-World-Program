# Feature Engineering Dictionary — Company A Telecom PoC

> **Task T5.2 & T5.3 (PRD §8.3):** Every engineered feature is documented below with its exact mathematical formula, business rationale grounded in EDA, leakage audit (confirming availability at prediction time before the 31–60 day churn observation window), and univariate AUC on the 70% training split.

| # | Feature Name | Formula (Safe Division with $\epsilon = 1.0$) | Business & Behavioral Rationale | Leakage Check (Available Pre-Window?) | Included in Final Model? |
|---:|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | `dev_age_gt_300` | $\mathbb{I}(\text{eqpdays} > 300)$ | Captures the sharp +23.8 pp churn jump once a subscriber's current handset crosses ~300 days. | **Pass:** `eqpdays` is current equipment age at observation date; threshold 300 derived on 70% train split. | **Yes** |
| 2 | `dev_age_301_360_peak` | $\mathbb{I}(300 < \text{eqpdays} \le 360)$ | Isolates the immediate post-300-day hazard peak (days 301–350, where sample churn reaches 59%–64%). | **Pass:** Observed at prediction time. | **Yes** |
| 3 | `tenure_le_9` | $\mathbb{I}(\text{months} \le 9)$ | Flags early-tenure subscribers still inside their initial 6–9 month honeymoon/commitment period (~31.8% churn). | **Pass:** `months` is current service tenure at observation date. | **Yes** |
| 4 | `tenure_10_12_cliff` | $\mathbb{I}(10 \le \text{months} \le 12)$ | Flags the first-year contract renewal cliff (months 10–12, peaking at 65.1% churn in month 11). | **Pass:** Observed at prediction time. | **Yes** |
| 5 | `eqp_per_tenure_day` | $\frac{\max(\text{eqpdays}, 0)}{\max(\text{months}, 1) \times 30}$ | Ratio of current device age to total account tenure (~1.0 for subscribers still on their original handset; $<0.5$ for subscribers who upgraded handsets). | **Pass:** Combines two current-state columns (`eqpdays`, `months`). | **Yes** |
| 6 | `mou_ratio_3m` | $\frac{\max(\text{mou\_Mean}, 0)}{\max(\text{avg3mou}, 0) + 1}$ | Measures short-term voice usage momentum relative to the prior 3-month average ($<1.0$ indicates declining engagement). | **Pass:** Uses observation-month mean vs. prior 3-month history. | **Yes** |
| 7 | `mou_ratio_6m` | $\frac{\max(\text{mou\_Mean}, 0)}{\max(\text{avg6mou}, 0) + 1}$ | Measures medium-term voice usage trajectory relative to the prior 6-month average. | **Pass:** Uses observation-month mean vs. prior 6-month history. | **Yes** |
| 8 | `rev_ratio_3m` | $\frac{\max(\text{rev\_Mean}, 0)}{\max(\text{avg3rev}, 0) + 1}$ | Captures recent bill expansion (bill shock) or revenue compression relative to the prior 3-month average. | **Pass:** Uses observation-month billing vs. prior 3-month history. | **Yes** |
| 9 | `rev_ratio_6m` | $\frac{\max(\text{rev\_Mean}, 0)}{\max(\text{avg6rev}, 0) + 1}$ | Captures medium-term revenue trajectory relative to the prior 6-month average. | **Pass:** Uses observation-month billing vs. prior 6-month history. | **Yes** |
| 10 | `drop_vce_rate` | $\frac{\max(\text{drop\_vce\_Mean}, 0)}{\max(\text{plcd\_vce\_Mean}, 0) + 1}$ | Dropped voice call rate per attempted voice call (network quality friction experienced by the user). | **Pass:** Historical call-quality metric. | **Yes** |
| 11 | `blck_vce_rate` | $\frac{\max(\text{blck\_vce\_Mean}, 0)}{\max(\text{plcd\_vce\_Mean}, 0) + 1}$ | Blocked voice call rate per attempted voice call (network congestion indicator). | **Pass:** Historical call-quality metric. | **Yes** |
| 12 | `unan_vce_rate` | $\frac{\max(\text{unan\_vce\_Mean}, 0)}{\max(\text{plcd\_vce\_Mean}, 0) + 1}$ | Unanswered voice call share per attempted call. | **Pass:** Historical call-behavior metric. | **Yes** |
| 13 | `completion_rate` | $\frac{\max(\text{complete\_Mean}, 0)}{\max(\text{attempt\_Mean}, 0) + 1}$ | Overall call completion ratio across voice and data attempts. | **Pass:** Historical call-quality metric. | **Yes** |
| 14 | `recurring_rev_share` | $\frac{\max(\text{totmrc\_Mean}, 0)}{\max(\text{rev\_Mean}, 0) + 1}$ | Share of monthly bill coming from fixed base recurring charge (plan stability vs. variable charges). | **Pass:** Historical billing composition. | **Yes** |
| 15 | `overage_rev_share` | $\frac{\max(\text{ovrrev\_Mean}, 0)}{\max(\text{rev\_Mean}, 0) + 1}$ | Share of monthly bill driven by overage penalties (high overage share indicates plan mismatch / bill shock). | **Pass:** Historical billing composition. | **Yes** |
| 16 | `custcare_per_call` | $\frac{\max(\text{custcare\_Mean}, 0)}{\max(\text{attempt\_Mean}, 0) + 1}$ | Customer-care call intensity relative to total calling activity (separates active callers from silent defectors). | **Pass:** Historical support interaction metric. | **Yes** |
| 17 | `mou_zero_flag` | $\mathbb{I}(\text{mou\_Mean} == 0)$ | Identifies the 1.6% zero-usage segment (`mou_Mean == 0`) with 79.8% churn ("silent defectors"). | **Pass:** Observed at prediction time. | **Yes** |
| 18 | `mou_le_10_flag` | $\mathbb{I}(\text{mou\_Mean} \le 10)$ | Identifies near-dormant accounts ($\le 10$ MOU/month, 65.4% churn). | **Pass:** Observed at prediction time. | **Yes** |
| 19 | `missing_change_mou_flag` | $\mathbb{I}(\text{change\_mou is NaN})$ | Preserves the high-risk missingness signal in `change_mou` ($n=891$, 76.1% churn). | **Pass:** Observed at prediction time. | **Yes** |
| 20 | `demo_missing_count` | $\sum_{c \in \text{Demo}} \mathbb{I}(c \text{ is NaN})$ | Counts missing demographic fields per customer (captures unverified/prepaid-like profiles). | **Pass:** Observed at prediction time. | **Yes** |
