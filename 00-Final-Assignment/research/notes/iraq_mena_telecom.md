# Topic Note 1 — Iraq & MENA Telecom Market Context (`research/notes/iraq_mena_telecom.md`)

## Key Verified Facts
1. **Market Saturation in Iraq (`[1]`, `[2]`):**
   - According to **DataReportal's *Digital 2024: Iraq* and *Digital 2025: Iraq*** (`[1]`), Iraq had **46.00 million** active cellular mobile connections in early 2024 (**100.0%** of the 46.01M population), growing to **48.1 million** connections in early 2025 (**103%** of the ~46.5M population).
   - According to the **World Bank / ITU World Development Indicators (`IT.CEL.SETS.P2`)** (`[2]`), Iraq's mobile cellular subscriptions reached **100.08 per 100 people** in 2024.
2. **MENA Regional Benchmarks & Prepaid Dominance (`[3]`):**
   - According to **GSMA Intelligence (*The Mobile Economy MENA 2024*)** (`[3]`), the MENA region reached **427 million** unique mobile subscribers (**64%** unique population penetration) and **49%** mobile internet penetration (~327M users).
   - Across Iraq and Levant markets, **85%–90%+ of connections are prepaid** (`[3]`, `[5]`), and multi-SIM ownership (carrying both a Zain Iraq and an Asiacell SIM to arbitrage coverage or data bundles) explains why total active SIM connections exceed 100% of the population while unique individual penetration sits near ~80%.

## Strategic Implication ("So What" for Slide 2)
When mobile connection penetration exceeds **100% of the population** (`[1]`, `[2]`), net-new ("greenfield") subscriber acquisition is exhausted. Every gross addition must be won either as a secondary SIM or by poaching a competitor's subscriber. Consequently, **protecting existing active subscribers and their monthly ARPU is the primary lever for EBITDA protection**.
